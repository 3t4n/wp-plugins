AISiteAssistant is an AI-powered chatbot designed to provide automated customer support using your website's content.

== Description ==
AISiteAssistant is an AI-powered chatbot that leverages the full knowledge base of your website to answer user queries. By collecting publicly available content from your website, our plugin creates a comprehensive knowledge base that powers the chatbot, enhancing user engagement and reducing the need for manual customer support.

**Please note:** This plugin collects publicly available content from your website and stores it securely on our servers to power the AI chatbot. No personal data from your site visitors is collected. By using this plugin, you agree to the [Terms of Service](https://aisiteassistant.com/terms-of-service) and [Privacy Policy](https://aisiteassistant.com/privacy/) of AISiteAssistant.

**Key Features:**
- AI-driven chatbot interface that interacts with visitors.
- Utilizes your website’s comprehensive knowledge base.
- Automatically answers customer queries based on your site's content.
- Easy integration and minimal configuration required.
- Subscription-based service with a 5-day money-back guarantee.
- Customizable chatbot behavior and responses.
- Support for multiple languages.
- Enhanced data security measures.
- Improved chatbot response time.
- Bug fixes and performance improvements.

== Installation ==
1. **Upload the Plugin:**
   - Upload the `aisiteassistant` folder to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory, or install the plugin via the WordPress Plugins screen directly.

2. **Activate the Plugin:**
   - Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' screen in WordPress.

3. **Create an AISiteAssistant Account:**
   - Visit [AISiteAssistant](https://aisiteassistant.com/) to create an account and subscribe to a suitable plan.

4. **Configure the Plugin:**
   - Navigate to the AISiteAssistant settings in your WordPress dashboard.
   - Enter your account credentials to connect the plugin with your AISiteAssistant account.
   - Customize the chatbot settings as per your requirements.

5. **Start Using AISiteAssistant:**
   - Once connected, the chatbot will be live on your website, ready to assist your visitors.

**Please Note:** By activating and using this plugin, you consent to the collection and processing of your website's publicly available content as outlined in our [Privacy Policy](https://aisiteassistant.com/privacy/).

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How does AISiteAssistant work? =
AISiteAssistant uses AI to process the publicly available content on your website and answer user queries based on that information. It interacts with users via a chatbot interface, providing real-time assistance.

= What data does AISiteAssistant collect? =
We collect publicly available content from your website by accessing raw HTML from your site's database. This includes pages, posts, and any other publicly accessible content necessary to build the chatbot's knowledge base.

= Does AISiteAssistant collect personal data from my site visitors? =
No, we do not collect any personal data from your site visitors. The chatbot operates solely using the publicly available content from your site.

= Where is the collected data stored? =
All collected content is stored securely on our servers at aisiteassistant.com. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

= How is my data used? =
The data is used exclusively to power the AI chatbot on your website, enabling it to provide accurate and helpful responses to your visitors' queries. We do not use your data for any other purposes.

= Is my data shared with third parties? =
No, we do not share your data with any third parties unless required by law. Payment processing is handled by Stripe, and they have their own [Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

= How secure is my data? =
Absolutely. We prioritize your data security and ensure that all interactions and information are handled securely in compliance with industry standards, including GDPR.

= Do I need coding skills to use AISiteAssistant? =
No, you do not need any coding skills. The plugin is designed for easy setup and configuration through the WordPress admin panel.

= What is the subscription model? =
AISiteAssistant operates on a subscription-based model. You can choose a plan that fits your needs on our [website](https://aisiteassistant.com/). We offer a 5-day money-back guarantee, and your card will not be charged before the trial period ends.

= How do I create an account and subscribe? =
Visit [AISiteAssistant](https://aisiteassistant.com/), sign up for an account, and select a subscription plan. After subscribing, use your account credentials to connect the plugin to your website.

= What happens after the 5-day money-back period? =
If you decide to continue using AISiteAssistant after the 5-day trial, your subscription will automatically be charged based on the plan you selected. If you are not satisfied, you can cancel within the 5-day period, and your card will not be charged.

= Can I upgrade or downgrade my subscription? =
Yes, you can manage your subscription plans directly through your AISiteAssistant account on our website.

= How do I request deletion of my data? =
You can request the deletion of your data at any time by contacting us via our [contact form](https://aisiteassistant.com/contact).
