== Description ==

# InstaCash Buy Now Pay Later

A payment service gateway to Woocommerce webshops that allows a customer to pay in installment to cover a purchase.

From the available online offers of the InstaCash Application, we get a calculated amount based on which we can start the application process.

== Installment Payment (BNPL) powered by instacash ==

- instacash installment payment is a customizable installment solution tailored to a merchant’s brand, designed for B2C customers.
- With installment payments, you can attract new customers and encourage repeat purchases from your existing clientele.
- Using a prequalification process available directly on the product page, your customers can find out in just 3 minutes whether they can pay for their chosen product in installments.

Acquire new customers and encourage your existing ones to make repeat purchases!

== Customer Support ==

Our customer support team is available daily via Messenger, email, and phone. You don’t need to handle any inquiries related to installment payments—our support team takes care of everything. We’re available every day from 9 AM to 6 PM to assist your customers with any questions they may have.

== White-Label Installment Payment ==

The instacash installment payment solution is tailored to your business and branded to appear seamlessly in your webshop. With bank-grade, innovative credit scoring and a fully digital, uninterrupted process, it ensures a smooth experience for your customers. Additionally, a merchant-specific installment account allows your customers to track their payments with ease.

== Prequalification ==

With prequalification initiated directly from the product page, your customers can find out in just 3 minutes if they’re eligible to pay for the chosen product in installments. This feature encourages customers to proceed with their purchase, significantly increasing the likelihood of conversion after completing the prequalification.

== Integration flow ==

1. Get and enable the plugin
2. Register on Merchant portal.
3. Our colleagues will contact you.
4. They help with the necessary settings and the creation of offers.
5. When you're ready, you'll see the new payment method appear.
