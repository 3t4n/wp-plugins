FastApps turns your responsive WordPress blog into a native mobile application

== Description ==

<a href=http://www.fastapps.mu>FastApps</a> turns your responsive website into a native mobile application

* Simply give us your responsive website url, Fastapps will generate a native mobile application.
* Download and install your mobile application on your phone and test it.
* Once fully satisfied, you can ask us to publish your app on the stores for a fee (check packages pricing).

FastApps is the only Wordpress plugin solution that gives for a ONE OFF fee (NO SUBSCRIPTION) a mobile native application including a push notification service and a manual publishing service.

You want to engage your readers and customers of your wordpress website on the app stores with a mobile native application offering push notifications ?  You don't really want spend thousands in mobile native application development (at least not now) ?

Fastapps is the simplest way to do it.
Upload the Fastapps plugin to your plugin directory, Activate it, then go to the Fastapps menu to proceed.

1, 2, 3: You're done!

Is it a real native mobile application ?
Yes. The app is a native software made with Android Java SDK or iOS Objective-C, including a push notification library, which loads your responsive website at app launch.

* For free users :
What’s included in the free service ?
You get an app which is a native mobile application made with Android Java SDK or iOS Objective-C.
The Android build can be downloaded and installed on any phone that allow unknown sources. You can share it to your friends or eventually put it on your website to be downloaded.

* For paid users :
Why should I use your paid service ?
Our paid model is one off only, NO SUBSCRIPTION. Your app is yours, for life. You get included a push notification interface from where you can send notifications to your app users and we publish your app for you if you want.

Please note that iOS applications are not available yet.

== Installation ==

== FAQ ==

* Why should I use your service ?
Our paid model is one off only, NO SUBSCRIPTION. Your app is yours, for life. You get included a push notification interface from where you can send notifications to your app users and we publish your app for you if you want.

* What is the 30 day money back garantee ?
Once you downloaded your build for testing you have 30 days to ask for a refund. Just click on refund and your paypal account will be refunded in less than 12 business hours. Note that once you request us to publish the app, you can not be refunded.

* Is it a real native mobile application ?
Yes. The app is a native software made with Android Java SDK or iOS Objective-C, including a push notification library, which loads your responsive website at app launch.

* Why should I use your service ?
Our paid model is one off only, NO SUBSCRIPTION. Your app is yours, for life. You get included a push notification interface from where you can send notifications to your app users and we publish your app for you if you want.

* Can the app work offline ?
No. It actually checks if a internet connection is available at startup.

* When the service will be available for iOS ?
Soon... we all love christmas ;) specially Christmas 2015...

* Can i publish my application myself ?
Yes for paid packages. For Android the application posseses a certificate that allow publishing on Google Play. You need a Google Play account (25$ per year) and follow Google Play instructions to publish your app.

* Do I need a Google Play account ?
No. We can publish on our on account. You don’t need to pay the Google Play annual fee of 25$ per year. Note that on the Google Play page the name of our company will appear.

* Can I customize my application ?
You can customize your responsive website, but can not customize the native part of the application.

* Do i get the native application source code ?
No.

* Are there any restrictions for my app content ?
Your content must be compatible with the stores guidelines. Your app might be rejected by the stores if the it doesn’t comply with their guidelines.

* How can I send push notifications ?
Access your private push notifications panel and send messages.

* How many push notifications can i send ?
You are limited to 1000 push notifications per month. If you need more ask us for a paid upgrade.

* The free package allows me to publish the app ?
No. Though the Android build can be downloaded and installed on any phone that allow unknown sources. You can share it to your friends or eventually put it on your website to be downloaded.

* Are there any restrictions if my app is published under your account ?
Yes. Your content and push notifications must comply with the store guidelines. We might unpublish it any time if there is any problem related to content or notifications. We don't allow simple Facebook pages, Youtube or bit.ly redirections, only full websites with a proper domain name are authorized.

* Can you publish my app for me ?
Yes for paid packages. We will publish it on your account or on ours upon your choice. Just send us all parameters (screenshots, description, categories, logo etc..) in your dashboard.

* What are the differences between publishing on my on account and publishing on mine ?
Nothing except on our account you will see our brand below as the app publisher.

* How to install an apk on my phone?
Simplest way is to download the app and send it via mail to your phone (to your gmail address setup on your phone for example). Please check that you can allow installation of unknown sources.

* Where can i download the wordpress plugin?
The wordpress plugin to turn your website into a mobile application is available <a href=http://www.fastapps.mu/fastapps-plugin.zip>here</a>

* What else should i know ?
If you pay a package, we give 5$ to local associations !
