== Description ==
Concord’s next-generation platform unifies consent management, privacy requests, and data mapping to help companies build trust, accelerate growth, and ensure real compliance with evolving data privacy and AI regulations including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, the EU AI Act, and many more.

### **Industry Leading Features**

- **Full Next-Generation Privacy Platform:** Real compliance with regulations is more than just cookie consent. Our unified approach to consent management, privacy requests, and data mapping helps businesses fully comply with privacy laws like GDPR, CCPA, DSGVO, PDPA, DPDPA, LGPD, PIPEDA, and more.
- **Modern Consent Management & Easy Installation:** Location and language aware cookie consent and privacy management with flexible consent tracking and logging. Our modern user experiences are easily customized for your brand via our no-code editor and deployed in minutes. The no-code approach to consent helps you quickly comply with global data privacy regulations.
- **Advanced Automatic Detection & Blocking Engine:** Stricter privacy regulations like GDPR require you to block all trackers (cookies, scripts, iframes, etc.) prior to user consent. Many cookie consent tools are not truly compliant with those regulations or require numerous ongoing manual technical maintenance steps to stay compliant. Our best-in-class engine processes cookies, scripts, and other trackers automatically to make real compliance as easy as possible.  An exhaustive library of trackers helps you stay compliant and real-time website scanning detects and categorizes new trackers.
- **Regions & Geo-Targeting**: Advanced control over end user experiences (Consent Banner, Privacy Center, etc.) shown to each individual user based on their current geographical location.
- **Automatic Language Translation**: Concord supports automatic multi-language translation in our Consent Banner and Unified Privacy Center (38+ languages and growing).
- **Advanced Privacy Controls**: We provide support a number of advanced privacy control options that are required by certain privacy regulations across the globe, including Global Privacy Control (GPC), multiple Do Not Sell/Share options, and Sensitive Data consent.
- **Flexible Universal Consent Tracking & Auditable Reports**: In addition to out-of-the-box consent capture, our powerful universal consent engine enables you to capture consent for any type of consent (terms of service, email, etc.). Auditable consent and privacy request logs ensure full traceability for compliance.
- **Google Consent Mode V2 & Google Tag Manager Template**: Adjust how and when data is shared with Google based on user consent collected via Concord\'s Consent Banner & Privacy Center. Our Google-approved Tag Manager template for Concord is also available via the Google Community Template Gallery.
- **Integrated Privacy Requests:** Our Unified Privacy Center experience includes streamlined and secure data privacy request forms and workflows to simplify the processing of regulation-required privacy requests/Data Subject Access Requests (DSARs). This includes fully configurable request forms, identity verification, and data system specific automations that work hand-in-hand with our data mapping capabilities.
- **Data Mapping:** A number of privacy regulations require certain companies to maintain a comprehensive inventory of all the data they have about people and how that data is used. Our guided data mapping experience makes it easy to add and document the systems that you currently use at your organization, enabling a single source of truth. Once your data systems are configured, they can then be used for internal reporting, compliance documentation, and the handling of data privacy requests.
- **Policy Generation:** Concord simplifies policy creation with pre-configured templates and automatic syncing of data, such as detected cookies, to ensure your business remains compliant with evolving regulatory requirements. Generate tailored cookie policies, privacy policies, terms of service, AI policies, and other critical documents to meet current and future needs.
- **Powerful Integrations**: Concord works across platforms, seamlessly integrating with a wide range of systems. We offer integrations for popular platforms like WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace, Wix, React, and more, ensuring flexibility and ease of use for any setup.

== Installation ==
### **Automatic Installation**

- From your WordPress dashboard, navigate to **Plugins** and click **Add New Plugin.**
- Search for “Concord” and click **Install Now.**
- Click **Activate** to enable the plugin.

### **Manual Installation**

- Download the Concord plugin zip file from the [WordPress.org](http://WordPress.org) page or via our [Concord Wordpress Integration Guide](https://www.concord.tech/docs/wordpress-concord-integration-guide).
- From your WordPress dashboard, go to **Plugins** and click **Add New Plugin**.
- Click **Upload Plugin**, choose the downloaded zip file, and click **Install Now**.
- Once installed, click **Activate** to enable the plugin.

### **Plugin Updates**

When an update is available, a notification will appear on the **Installed Plugins** page. Update directly from the WordPress dashboard to ensure you’re using the latest version. Keeping the plugin up-to-date is strongly recommended to maintain compatibility and access new features.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==
**Does Concord meet various privacy laws like GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, DSGVO, PDPA, DPDPA, etc.?**

- Yes, Concord was built to work with different regions and privacy laws. We provide full regional support that detects the location of each user and that can be used to show different experiences that align with different privacy laws. Concord also includes some additional more advanced features not typically included by standard cookie consent vendors, including privacy request/data subject request handling and data mapping. That provides additional compliance functionality allowing you to check more compliance boxes off.

**Can we change the look of the consent banner and privacy center to match our company branding? What customization options do you support?**

- Branding is fully configurable and we support multiple options to match Concord to the look of your website (bar/box banner type, location, colors, logo, font, light/dark/branded theme, button radius, card radius, etc.). More details can be found under the User Experiences section in Concord and in our documentation here: [Custom Branding](https://www.concord.tech/docs/setting-up-custom-branding-logos-colors) or in our Getting Started Guide here: [Getting Started](https://www.concord.tech/docs/getting-started).

**Does it automatically detect and block new trackers as they appear?**

- Concord has always focused on providing the most powerful automatic tracker scanning, categorization, and blocking engine in the industry. There are unfortunately too many other consent products out there that are simply not compliant or that require numerous ongoing manual technical maintenance steps to stay compliant. We automatically detect and block new trackers in real-time as they appear and they are matched against a large global library. If we don\'t have a match, it will appear in your trackers table (cookies, scripts, iframes, etc.) for categorization.

**Does Concord support Google Consent Mode V2? Is Concord a Google CMP partner?**

- Yes, Concord is a certified Google CMP partner and Concord fully supports Google Consent Mode V2. Google Consent Mode V2 allows websites to adjust how and when data is shared with Google based on user consent collected via the Concord Consent Banner & Privacy Center.

**With Concord’s multi-language automatic translation and regional compliance features, how does the platform handle localized consent management across different regions with varying privacy laws, such as the EU\'s GDPR?**

- Concord currently offers geo-targeting and automatic language translation of our Consent Banner and Privacy Center, and we have separate engines for each. For multi-language automatic translation, we use each user\'s preferred language so that the consent banner and privacy center are shown in their desired language. For regional compliance support, we detect user location in real-time and allow you to create multiple regions (like having one for EU GDPR and one for the US) with different settings and experiences.
