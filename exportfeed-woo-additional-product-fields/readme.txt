ExportFeed: Woo Additional Fields Plugin to Add Brand, MPN, UPC, EAN & Shipping Rates

== Description ==
<P>
Adding extra fields to your WooCommerce products is necessary to provide additional information to customers. You need to add some product information without failing if you are exporting your products to marketplaces like Amazon, Google, eBay etc. This plugin allows you to add such mandatory information to your WooCommerce products so you can properly list your products to the marketplaces without getting errors.

<H3>What fields can you add to your WooCommerce products using this plugin?
<ul>
<li>Brand value to your Simple and Variable products</li>
<li>UPC value to your WooCommerce products</li>
<li>MPN value to your WooCommerce products</li>
<li>EAN value to your WooCommerce products</li>
<li>Shipping cost to your WooCommerce products</li>
<li>Notes to your WooCommerce products</li>
</ul>
<P>
<H3>Why should you add the Brand value to your WooCommerce products?
<P>
When listing bulk products on most of the popular marketplaces like Google, Amazon etc, Brand is a mandatory value without which, your product listings will create errors. So, why is brand a mandatory value? It helps in managing products under the brand taxonomy. And, this also has an impact on the conversion value of your products on the marketplaces. Brand is a searchable attribute and it carries the goodwill value also so don’t miss to add it.
Adding Brand is supported for:
<ul>
<li>Simple products</li>
<li>Variation products</li>
</ul>
<H3>Why should you add the Unique Product Identifiers?
<P>
Unique product identifiers are used to ensure the accurate identification of products and variations in order to make an accurate listing on the marketplaces. Google shopping has defined these values as mandatory for almost all product category. In order to successfully upload your products to Google Shopping, you should either provide Brand and UPC or Brand and MPN values for your products.
<P>
Alike for Amazon, you need to provide either the UPC value or if you have GTIN exemption, you need to associate the Brand name exempted for GTIN to create a successful listing.
<ul>
<li>UPC - Unique Product Code of 12 digits that identifies the products globally</li>
<li>MPN - Manufacturer Part Number required if you are selling parts of products</li>
<li>EAN - European Article Number of 13 digits that identifies the products uniquely</li>
</ul>

<P>
Note: This plugin does not display the added data on your frontend, rather it works to add those data in your product feed.

== Installation ==

>[**HOW TO GUIDE**](http://www.exportfeed.com/tos) |||
>[** ALL FAQS **](http://www.exportfeed.com/faq/)
<P>
A few quick steps<P>
1) Just download it through this page and upload it to your plugins folder or from your WordPress backend, click on Add New plugin like you would normally add any other plugins.<BR>
2) Activate the plugin and go to All Products submenu on your WooCommerce sub-menu.<BR>
3) Now, select the products for which you want to add the extra values on WooCommerce.<BR>
4)Save the product and follow the same process for all of the products you want to add values.<BR>
