== Description ==
AxiomaBot: Trained on your data, powered by LLMs - instant access to the knowledge you need. Build a custom GPT, embed it on your website and let it handle customer support, lead generation, engage with your users, and more.
With this plugin, you can embed your [AxiomaBot](https://axiomabot.com/) chatbot directly into your Wordpress site.
This plugin utilizes Axioma AI services to deploy a pre-trained chatbot on your website, leveraging the data you've uploaded to the Axioma Bot application. For more information on [privacy](https://axiomabot.com/privacy-policy/), and [terms of service](https://axiomabot.com/terms-of-service/), please refer to our dedicated sections on these topics.
== Frequently Asked Questions ==
= How do I initialize my chatbot? =
1. Install this plugin to your WordPress website.
2. Copy your Chatbot ID from [AxiomaBot](https://app.axiomabot.com/) chatbot settings tab.
3. Navigate to "Settings" tab under the "Axiomabot" menu in the sidebar of your WordPress dashboard.
4. Paste your Chatbot ID into the designated field.
5. Click "Save Changes".
