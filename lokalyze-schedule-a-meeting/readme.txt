A Single Way to schedule a meeting with your team member. 

== Description ==

Increase appointments, customers, sales, and revenue from your WordPress website with this online scheduling plugin. It offers a self-service scheduling system for 1-on-1 meetings for your WordPress website.

Ideal for a business-to-consumer service like consultants, accountants, attorneys, medical practices, and wellness centers, this plugin enables prospects needing your services to schedule a meeting on your website.

<strong>Convert website visitors to prospects or customers</strong>

<ul>
	<li>Take online appointment requests</li>
	<li>Enable online bookings and appointments</li>
	<li>Give your website visitors the ability to book an appointment or schedule a meeting</li>
</ul>

<strong>Main Schedule-a-Meeting features</strong>

<ul>
	<li>Business booking calendar for you and your business</li>
	<li>Unlimited bookings and services list</li>
	<li>All types of services online, including 1-on-1 meetings, classes or events</li>
	<li>Customizable business hours and preferences</li>
	<li>Customizable booking form</li>
	<li>Adjusts to any time zone</li>
</ul>

<strong>Calendar Bookings</strong>

Let people book an appointment with you or your partners or employees by submitting an email with a preferred time to meet with you. The system automatically sends you an email informing you of the booked appointment. 

Amongst the information collected from the prospect is their email address and phone number so that you contact them directly to confirm your appointment.

<strong>Customize Your Booking Form</strong>

The Schedule-a-meeting form can be customized to include additional information that you need to book your appointment.

You can then communicate with customers before approving their booking based upon the information provided to you within the booking form.

The booking form is a great way to ensure that you have all of the information you need before finalizing the booking request.

<strong>Easily manage your bookings</strong>

The appointment booking calendar integrates into any WordPress website. The calendar booking software has a user-friendly design and interface that does away with the back-and-forth communication associated with booking an appointment.

<strong>Different options to conduct your meeting</strong>

Once your meeting is booked, you can either conduct your session by phone, in ZOOM, or person. 

Schedule as many meetings as you would like and send the ZOOM information or phone number by email.

<strong>Schedule-a-Meeting with Appointments plugin.</strong>

<strong>Excellent and professional booking experience:</strong> The Schedule-a-meeting plugin has a modern and customizable user interface and is the best alternative for managing online appointments.

<strong>Manage your availability through the Calendar:</strong> Define booking periods using predefined dates in the calendar.

<strong>Manage session lengths and your availability</strong> – Scheduling options allow you to define your availability in 15- or 30-minute increments easily

<strong>Send Appointment Reminders:</strong> Send appointment reminders to the customer before your scheduled appointment.

<strong>Mobile - friendly</strong> – The Schedule-a-Meeting plugin is fully responsive and resizes on mobile devices, making it look great on mobile and desktop devices.

<strong>What happens when a new booking appointment is submitted?</strong>

When a prospect or customer submits their booking, you receive an email that includes all of the information that the customer submitted on the Schedule-a-Meeting booking form. 
You can then book your appointment for a phone, ZOOM, or in-person meeting.

== Installation ==

1. On the admin page click on the 'Plugins' menu.
2. Click on the 'Add New' button.
3. Click on the 'Search' link.
4. Fill 'Schedule A Meeting' into the search field.
5. Click on the 'Search Plugins' button.
6. Find the 'Lokalyze - Schedule A Meeting' plugin in the result list.
7. Click on the 'Install Now' link.
8. Click on the 'Activate plugin' link.

OR

1. Download the installation package (.zip file) to your computer.
2. On the admin page click on the 'Plugins' menu.
3. Click on the 'Add New' button.
4. Click on the 'Upload' link.
5. Click on the 'Browse...' button then find the installation package (.zip file) on your computer.
6. Click on the 'Install Now' button.
7. Click on the 'Activate plugin' link.
