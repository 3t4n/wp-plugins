== Description ==
Survey's lets you know what your visitors are thinking, so to be smart download "Google Analytics Post Survey widget" for WordPress and know their feedback.

Google analytics post survey is a smartest plugin which helps you to find your customers feedback and thoughts on your post. == Installation ==
Upload the GA Post Survey Widget folder to the /wp-content/plugins/directory

Activate the plugin through the \'Plugins\' menu in WordPress

== Frequently Asked Questions ==
= Why to use this plugin? =

This plugin helps website owners to know about your customer's feedback on your posts and also helps to find the quality of your posts so that you can easily improve your content.


= Do you require google analytics account? =

Yes, to use this plugin you should have a google analytics account to check your survey results.

= Does this plugin support all themes? =

Yes, absolutely it support all the WordPress themes. If you didn't work on your theme just inform us. We'll help to resolve it the next version and we'll update it.

= Does it affect your plugin and themes? =

No, it doesn't affect your plugin and themes. It a very light weighted plugin and doesn't affect other files and codes on your site.  So you don't have to worry about your themes or plugins after installing this plugin.

= What about support? =

I am constantly improving this plugin. So you won't find much errors. If you want to file a support ticket then just create a support ticket at WordPress forum and I will work on that issues as fast as I can and get the better results for you.
