== Installation ==

1. Upload the [online_marketing_free_rolls] directory and everything in it to your [/wp-content/plugins/] directory.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress.
3. Click 'Settings' -> 'Free Poker Rolls'.
4. Follow the easy instructions from there.
