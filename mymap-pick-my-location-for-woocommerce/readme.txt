This plugin speed up checkout with automatic address filling on checkout page and share location URL with order using google map API.

== Description ==

Developed by [Czargroup Technologies](https://czargroup.net) | [Demo](https://marketplace.czargroup.net/mymap-demo/) | [Documents](https://marketplace.czargroup.net/documentation/)

MiMap offers smooth integration with WooCommerce, providing effortless address auto-fill based on your current latitude and longitude. Along with the order, a Google Maps-based URL is generated, ensuring hassle-free and timely shipping.

**Key Benefits of MiMap:**
- Streamlined address auto-fill for faster checkout.
- Precise latitude and longitude details for accurate delivery.
- Google Maps-based URL attached to every order for easy tracking and delivery coordination.

After an order is placed, the attached Google Maps URL enables shipping and logistics teams to deliver seamlessly and on time, ensuring accurate and hassle-free delivery to the customer's exact location.

Choose MiMap for an efficient and reliable e-commerce solution tailored to meet your delivery needs!

== External Services ==

This plugin connects to the Google Maps API to provide location-related functionality, such as auto-filling addresses and determining the user's current location. This integration is essential for the plugin's core functionality.

- **What data is sent and when:**
  - When the "Pick My Location" button is clicked, the user's current location (latitude and longitude) is sent to the Google Maps API to fetch the corresponding address.
  - No additional user data is sent to the API.

- **Service provider:**
  - The service is provided by Google LLC.

- **Links to the service's terms and privacy policies:**
  - [Google Maps API Terms of Service](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms)
  - [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

Users are encouraged to review the terms of service and privacy policy to understand how their data is handled by the Google Maps API.
