== Description ==
### Contact Form Counter
The Contact Form Counter plugin extends the functionality of the popular “Contact Form 7” plugin, offering administrators powerful tools to add counters, timestamps, and time values to email content, subject lines, and even frontend pages. This innovative plugin ensures unique identification for every email or displayed information, enhancing both usability and customization.

= Key Features : =

* **Advanced Integration with Contact Form 7:**  
  * Seamlessly integrates with Contact Form 7 to provide added functionality for email communications and frontend displays.

* **Customizable Shortcode :**  
  * Use the shortcode in email subjects, email content, or on frontend pages.  
  * Options to switch between Counter, Time, or Timestamp modes.

* **Prefix and Custom Length :**  
  * Add a custom text prefix to counters for better readability and identification.  
  * Configure the number of digits for counters (e.g., `00000009`).

* **Unique Email Identification :**  
  * Automatically appends counters, timestamps, or time values to email subjects and body content, ensuring every email is distinct.  
  * Ideal for scenarios where email tracking or unique references are required.

* **Frontend Display :**  
  * Showcase the counter, time, or timestamp directly on your website for visitors using the shortcode on any page.

* **User-Friendly Controls :**  
  * Simple admin settings to configure counters, prefixes, and display formats effortlessly.

This plugin is an essential tool for websites that rely on Contact Form 7 for email interactions and want to enhance email uniqueness, tracking, or display additional dynamic information on their website.
Transform the way you manage emails and frontend displays with the Contact Form Counter plugin!

== Installation ==
1. Upload the plugin `Contact Form Counter` to the directory `/wp-content/plugins/`
2. Activate the plugin from the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress.

You will find 'Contact Form Counter' menu in your WordPress admin panel's 'Settings' menu.
