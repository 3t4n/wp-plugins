**ABOUT THE FLOATING TIKTOK BUTTON / TIKTOK FOLLOW BUTTON**

This Floating tiktok button plugin allows you to display a static/animated floating Tiktok button (logo) or your Tikcode (on frontend) linked to your Tiktok account to increase your Tiktok followers.

Step 1:

- Select what to display on frontend (Floating Tiktok button, your Tikcode, both, ...)
- Enter your Tiktok username/ID
- Select where to display these floating buttons
- Select how to display these buttons (desktop / mobile)

Step 2:

- Configure the style of your floating Tiktok button (satic or animated)
- You can use your own Tiktok image

Step 3:

- Customize your floating tiktok button
  - Button text
  - Font
  - Border
  - Width/height
  - ...

Step 4:

- Customize the position of your floating buttons

And you're done.

**ABOUT THE TIKTOK QRCODE -- TIKCODE**

Did you know every TikTok user has a TikCode that other users can scan to quickly find their profile on the video-sharing platform? The QR Code helps you get people to find your profile on the app and makes them free from the task of manually locating which profile is yours.

**The TikCode will be of great help to you.** Arguably, if you are an active person on various social networks, using these TikCode will allow you to gain more followers on TikTok quickly.

This plugin can be used with our other Tiktok plugin: Add tiktok pixel for Tiktok Apps

== Installation ==

= Installing manually =

1. Unzip all files to the `/wp-content/plugins/floating-tiktok-button` directory
2. Log into WordPress admin and activate the 'Floating TikTok Button' plugin through the 'Plugins' menu
3. Go to "Floating TikTok Button" in the left-hand menu to start work on it.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How to find your Tiktok Qr Code (TikCode) =

It's not required to provide anything. Simply enter your Tiktok Username/ID, and our plugin will display your TikCode from your Tiktok account.

= Can I use a QR code Scanner to Scan a My TikCode? =

Yes. You can use a QR code Scanner or TikTok built-in scanner to Scan a Tikcode.

= How to add my Tiktok profile URL ? =

Just enter your Tiktok Username/ID. If you want to link to another account, enter another Tiktok Username/ID.
