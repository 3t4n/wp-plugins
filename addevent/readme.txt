== Description == 

The AddEvent Calendar Plugin for WordPress is a powerful and user-friendly tool to showcase upcoming events, conferences, schedules, calendars, webinars, and more—seamlessly integrating with your website’s design.

Don't have an AddEvent account yet? Sign up for your own [AddEvent Calendar](https://addevent.com/signup?utm_source=wordpress&utm_medium=plugin&utm_campaign=embeddable-calendar&utm_term=addevent-calendar&utm_content=signup). 

Embed a beautiful calendar on your website - display your events and keep your visitors up to date without any extra effort on their part.

AddEvent's Embeddable Calendar plugin lets you display your AddEvent calendar on your website. Install the plugin, add the AddEvent Calendar block, and put in the CalendarID of your AddEvent Calendar.

**Key Features:**
✅ Easy Embedding – Add beautiful, fully responsive event calendars anywhere on your site.
✅ Customization Options - Customize the calendar through block settings such as inclusion or removal of the calendar name, print button, timezone selector, subscribe buttons, and so forth
✅ Subscribe Button – Let visitors save your calendar feed in their calendar of choice (Google, Outlook, Apple, etc.).
✅ Real-Time Updates – Automatically sync event changes without needing manual updates to your website calendar. Events that are added and updated in AddEvent will reflect on your website in real time.
✅ Timezone Support – Ensure event times display accurately for global audiences. 
✅ Calendar Display Settings - Supports various calendar views (month, week, schedule)

Perfect for businesses, nonprofits, educators, coaches, marketers, and anyone hosting events who wants to share their schedule and calendar on their website! AddEvent’s WordPress plugin makes calendar sharing simple, professional, and effective. Start engaging your audience today!

👉 **Install now and turn your website into an event hub!**

1. Upload the plugin files to the `/wp-content/plugins/addevent` directory, or install the plugin directly through the WordPress plugins screen.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' screen in WordPress.

== External Services == 

This plugin integrates with the **AddEvent Calendar** to display and manage custom event calendars.

- **Service Used:** [AddEvent](https://www.addevent.com/?utm_source=wordpress&utm_medium=plugin&utm_campaign=embeddable-calendar&utm_term=AddEvent&utm_content=services-used)
- **What it Does:** This service allows embedding and managing event calendars on your WordPress site.
- **Data Sent:** When users interact with the calendar, requests are sent to AddEvent’s servers to fetch calendar events.
- **When is Data Sent:** Data is transmitted when the calendar is loaded on the webpage.
- **Where is Data Sent:** The requests go to `https://cdn.addevent.com/libs/cal/js/cal.embed.t1.init.js`.

== Frequently Asked Questions == 

= Where do I find my Calendar ID? = 

Navigate to your [AddEvent account](https://app.addevent.com/calendars), hover over your calendar name, and click the three dots. From there, click **Calendar Page**, and your Calendar ID can be found in the top calendar details section. 

= How do I configure the Calendar ID? = 

When you add the Add Event block, you can set the Calendar ID in the block's settings panel in the editor.

= Can I manage events directly in WordPress? = 

The plugin uses an external service [AddEvent] (https://www.addevent.com/?utm_source=wordpress&utm_medium=plugin&utm_campaign=embeddable-calendar&utm_term=AddEvent&utm_content=faq) for event management and is not manageable from WordPress.

= How do I uninstall the plugin? = 

Deactivate and delete the plugin from the **Plugins** menu. All related data will be removed upon deletion.
