== Description == 
Introducing the **ProSeller AI - AI Product Content Creator and Optimizer for WooCommerce**, a powerful plugin designed to boost your sales and conversion rates by generating SEO-optimized product titles, descriptions, short descriptions, and variation descriptions that will be more attractive to your users. Powered by the advanced AI of **ChatGPT**, this plugin allows you to effortlessly create compelling product descriptions tailored to your WooCommerce products.

Simply input your keywords, and the **ProSeller AI - AI Product Content Creator and Optimizer for WooCommerce** will generate unique, high-quality product titles, descriptions, short descriptions, and variation descriptions that enhance your store's SEO, making your products more discoverable. The plugin's smart algorithms ensure that every description is optimized for search engines based on the keywords you provide, helping your products rank higher and attract more customers.

Whether you're looking for an **AI WooCommerce product description writer** or a tool to streamline your content creation process, this plugin is the perfect solution for generating professional, keyword-optimized content with ease.

The **ProSeller AI - AI Product Content Creator and Optimizer for WooCommerce** plugin uses AI to create compelling SEO-optimized titles, descriptions, short descriptions, and variation descriptions to help improve your sales and conversion rates.

== Features: == 
* Automatically generate SEO-friendly titles, descriptions, short descriptions, and variation descriptions based on your keywords.
* You can modify any existing titles, descriptions, short descriptions, and variation descriptions using their current content as a prompt.
* Tailor content for higher sales and conversion rates.
* Re-write your existing descriptions and experiment with improved descriptions and titles.
* Powered by **AI** and **ChatGPT** for high-quality content creation.
* Seamlessly integrates with WooCommerce to enhance your store's product visibility.
* **Bulk Content Creation**: Generate content for multiple products at once. Select multiple products from the WooCommerce product listing page and generate content (titles, descriptions, short descriptions) for them in bulk.
* Easy content generation for selected fields: Define which fields to generate (e.g., title, description, short description) for each selected product.

Unlock the full potential of your WooCommerce store with this powerful **ProSeller AI - AI Product Content Creator and Optimizer for WooCommerce** tool!

== Frequently Asked Questions ==  
= How do I use the plugin? =  
You can generate product content by navigating to the product edit page.

= Where can I get support? =  
Support is available through the plugin support page.

= Is the plugin free? =  
Yes, the plugin is free to use. But you must use your own API key.  

= Do I need an API Key? =  
Yes, you will need to use your own OpenAI API. This is simple to create. You can follow the instructions on the [OpenAI website](https://platform.openai.com/docs/quickstart). However, the basic steps are as follows:

1. Log in or sign up to the OpenAI platform.
2. Go to the API Keys page.
3. Click Create new secret key.
4. Name your API key.
5. Copy your key to your clipboard.
6. Paste the key in the plugin settings.

= Does the Plugin only work on WooCommerce Products? =  
Yes, we focus on eCommerce and this plugin focuses on improving your products for better sales and conversions.

== Installation ==  
1. Upload the `ai-product-content-creator-for-woocommerce` folder to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.  
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress.  
3. Configure settings under 'Proseller AI' in the admin dashboard.
