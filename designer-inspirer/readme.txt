Song lyrics are nice enough, but to really get value out of the quotes we see in Wordpress each and every day, it's important to see something inspiring rather than vapid. 

== Description ==

Famous designers are often incredibly quotable, and we've taken some of the best quotes from the most famous designers in the world. Hopefully, they'll be able to inspire you to new heights in your Wordpress web design projects.
