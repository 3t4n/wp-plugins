A powerful **coupon plugin** for **affiliate marketers** and **bloggers** to create responsive and customizable coupon and deal boxes in WordPress.

== Description ==

**Coupon Plugin** is a flexible and lightweight **WordPress plugin** that enables **affiliate marketers** and bloggers to display **coupon codes** and **deals** on their websites. Easily create attractive and responsive **coupon boxes**, list coupons with shortcodes, and integrate affiliate links for monetization.

**Perfect for Affiliate Marketing & Bloggers:**

- Easily manage and display **affiliate coupons**.
- Promote your **affiliate deals** and **discount codes** on your WordPress site.
- Enhance **SEO** by optimizing coupon boxes for search engines.

<h3>FEATURES OF COUPON PLUGIN LITE</h3>

• 100% Responsive and Attractive Design for all devices
• Hide Coupon Functionality to show or hide deals based on conditions
• Store Image Functionality to display logos of coupon stores
• Coupon Expiry Date with Time to manage the validity of deals
• Inbuilt Affiliate Link System for tracking your earnings
• Stylish Popular Coupon Strip to highlight trending coupons
• List Coupons with a Shortcode for easy display anywhere
• Categorized Coupons by Store or Category for better organization

<h3>FEATURES OF COUPON PLUGIN PRO</h3>

• List Coupons By Category with a Shortcode to group deals by category
• List Coupons By Store with a Shortcode to display deals for specific stores
• **Affiliate Link Cloaking** for better tracking and aesthetics
• Coupon Voting to allow users to rate deals
• **Coupon Sharing** on Social Media (Facebook & Twitter)
• Coupon Popup for promotions and timed offers
• **Click to Copy Coupon** feature for easy user access
• Shortcode Grid Look for more structured display of coupons
• Insert Shortcodes Directly from the Editor for faster management

Running a **coupon website** or **affiliate marketing** site on WordPress has never been easier or more SEO-friendly. The **Coupon Plugin** helps you create attractive, fully responsive, and customizable coupon boxes that not only improve user experience but also drive more affiliate sales.

== Installation ==

**Installation Process 1:**

1. Download the plugin from the WordPress Plugin Repository and unzip the `coupon-lite.zip` file.
2. Upload the folder to your `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.
3. Activate the plugin via the 'Plugins' menu in your WordPress dashboard.

**Installation Process 2:**

1. In your WordPress dashboard, go to the 'Add New' section under Plugins.
2. Search for **'Coupon Plugin'**.
3. Click **'Install Now'** and then **Activate**.
