= Features =

**Flexible Generation of Any Fields**

Title: Generate titles based on the article, sections, or use the exact topic value (useful for creating databases, glossaries, directories, etc.).

Article Body: Generate the body in one go, by sections (first generate sections and then the body based on them), or custom sections (input exact headings using variables for topics and keywords, applying this structure to thousands of articles. Ideal for databases, glossaries, directories, etc.).

Categories and Tags: Specify existing categories or generate a specified number based on the article.

Images: Over 30+ presets and additional prompts.

Excerpt: Generate SEO-friendly excerpts based on the article.

Custom Fields and Taxonomies: Fully compatible with custom taxonomies and fields, allowing the generation of any custom field types.

Publication Date: Choose how articles will be published: instantly, scheduled for a specific date, or one per day starting next week? The plugin can handle it all.

**Auto Content Creation:** Automate Blog & Socia Medial
Fully automate your WordPress blog and social media with powerful auto content creation based on RSS feeds. Custom schedules, SEO optimization, and full control – keep your audience engaged with fresh updates like never before. [Auto Content Creation for WordPress](https://aiwuplugin.com/auto-content-creation-for-wordpress/ "Auto Content Creation for WordPress") uses fresh industry news as a trigger to auto-generate blog articles and social media posts for platforms like LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Facebook, and Instagram.

**Various Generation Modes:** Choose how the body of the article will be generated

Single-prompt Article: [Create WordPress post](https://aiwuplugin.com/ai-powered-wordpress-post-creation/ "Create WordPress post") in one step based on the title.

Sectioned Article Generation: Generates the body by first creating an outline and then generating each section individually.

Custom Sectioned Article Generation: Manually input an outline using variables like {topic}, {keyword1}, {keyword2}, etc., and generate each section based on this custom outline before combining them into the full body.

**Mass Article Generation:** [Bulk AI article generation mode](https://aiwuplugin.com/bulk-ai-article-generation/ "Bulk AI article generation mode") allows you to generate multiple articles in a single operation, ideal for large-scale content creation. You can create 10, 100, 1000, or an unlimited number of articles simultaneously while controlling each step of the generation process to ensure the quality of each post.

**Full Control and Advanced Settings:** The plugin provides control over every aspect, even when creating thousands of articles simultaneously:

Configure API and other generation behavior in a user-friendly interface.

Choose how each field is generated.

Input additional prompts for each field and the article as a whole.

Modify and improve default prompts to suit your needs.

Manage all operations in the History section, allowing for bulk management of operations.

**Smart Content Crosslinking** Enables the bulk integration of internal and external links into existing content. AI analyzes each article to create relevant additional paragraphs containing links to existing articles or external resources.

**Bulk Field Generation for Existing Posts:** Efficiently edit and update multiple article details across your site in one action, reducing manual work.
